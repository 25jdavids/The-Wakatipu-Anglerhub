Tips and Tricks

The Key to the Way of the Wakatipu

Learn about bait, tackle, lures and fishing techniques to help you get the most out of your fishing trips around the Wakatipu Basin.

🪱 Choosing the Right Bait

Choosing the right bait for the species you are targeting can make a big difference. Different fish may respond better to different types of bait, so think about the species you are targeting before heading out blindly especially with the clever species of trout and salmon.

Natural bait and artificial options can both be useful depending on the location and fishing conditions. Check out the regulations for the waterway you are fishing before using any bait.

🎣 Match Your Lure to the Conditions

Lures come in many shapes, sizes and colours. Try choosing a lure that suits the water conditions and visibility.

In clearer water, more natural-looking presentations may be useful, while different colours can be easier for fish to notice in darker or less clear conditions.

🪝 Hooks and Tackle

Make sure your hooks and other tackle are suitable for the fish you are targeting. Your tackle should be in good condition and checked regularly for damage.

The recommended hook size when using soft baits in the lake is a #7 or #8 hook. This hook is the best for trout becasue it remains hidden in the lure and allows for the lure to take more action out under the water.

Always carry spare tackle because the rocky environments and deep waterways always result in lost equipment You dont want to get stuck staring at a bunch of fish with no tackle.

🎣 Choosing a Rod and Reel

Choose a rod and reel combination that suits your fishing style and the type of water you are fishing. Queenstowns local fishing store Hunting And Fishing stocks a range of products for people of all age groups and skillsets for value and fair prices.

A setup used for a small stream may feel very different from one used for casting from the shoreline of a large lake such as Lake Wakatipu.

🧵 Check Your Line

Your fishing line is an important part of your setup. Check it regularly for wear, knots or damage before fishing.

Replace damaged line when necessary and make sure your knots are secure before casting.

The recommended beginner monofilament line to use is 8 lbs with a 15 lbs braid leader

🎒 Essential Tackle to Bring

Before heading out, make sure you have the equipment you need for your planned fishing trip.

Useful items may include:

    Fishing rod and reel

    Suitable bait or lures

    Hooks and spare tackle

    Extra fishing line

    A small tackle box

    A way to safely remove hooks

    A small net to land fish safely and prevent harm

    Suitable clothing for the weather

    Water and any other essentials for your trip

    Have a look at our maps page to plan out your trip and see if there are any destinations or facilities nearby if you dont plan on taking out your own food, accesories

🌊 Adjust to the Fishing Location

The Wakatipu Basin features a variety of fishing environments, including large lakes, smaller lakes, rivers and streams.

Consider the water depth, current, weather and access when choosing your bait and tackle. A setup that works well in one location may not be the best choice somewhere else. So its good to explore different types of fishing and depths to see what works for you

🌤 Check Conditions Before Fishing

Weather and water conditions can affect your fishing trip. Check the forecast before leaving and be prepared for changing conditions around Lake Wakatipu.

You can view the weather conditions on our websites weather page

🐟 Know Your Target Fish

Before choosing your bait and tackle, know what species you are hoping to catch. Visit the Fishing Spots page to see the different fish listed for locations around the Wakatipu Basin.

Visit the fishing spots page to search for certain fish at certain spots

🧹 Respect the Environment

Always take rubbish, old fishing line and unwanted tackle home with you. Help keep Lake Wakatipu and the surrounding waterways clean and safe for wildlife and future anglers.

📋 Check the Regulations

Rules can apply to fishing methods, equipment, bait and the species you are targeting. Always check the current official regulations before fishing.
