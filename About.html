Your personal guide to fishing within and around the Wakatipu basin, whether you're a seasoned angler or just trying out something new, we've got you covered with all the information you need to get the most out of your fishing adventures.

Our Inspiration

The Wakatipu Anglerhubs inspiration comes from the beautiful fishing opportunities around Queenstown mainly Lake Wakatipu. Many people struggle to find reliable information about where to fish, the types of gear to use, and when conditions are at their best. This website keeps all of that information in one net which makes it the set hook for all of the local and visiting fisherman.
