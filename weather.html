Local Weather Conditions

Check the conditions before casting out your lines

Weather can have a major effect on fishing around Lake Wakatipu. Use the below information to help best plan for a day out on the lake

🎣 Best Conditions for Fishing

Calm or lightly windy conditions can make it easier to fish from the shoreline and spot activity near the water.

Cloudy conditions can also be useful because fish may be more comfortable feeding away from deeper cover.

💨 Wind

Lake Wakatipu can experience strong winds, especially in open areas. Strong winds can make casting difficult and may create rough water.

Always check the forecast and choose a sheltered location when conditions become windy.

🌧 Rain and Water Levels

Rain can affect water clarity and river flow. After heavy rain, streams and rivers may become faster and more difficult to fish.

Take extra care around changing water conditions and slippery shorelines.

🧥 What to Bring

Weather around the Wakatipu Basin can change quickly. Bring suitable warm and waterproof clothing, especially when fishing early in the morning or later in the evening.
